Gif Me A Sec

Welcome to Gif Me A Sec 👋

Drop a reaction GIF on your webcam for a few seconds in any web meeting. To search for GIFs, add a free API key from at least one of these services. You can add the others later in Settings.

Where should it work?

🔒 Everything stays on your computer

When you turn it on, Chrome asks to let Gif Me A Sec "read and change your data" on the sites you picked. That's only so it can show GIFs on your camera there. It doesn't read the pages you visit, their URLs or their content, and it sends no data anywhere. Your keys and settings are saved only in this browser.

Gif Me A Sec isn't allowed on any meeting site yet.
