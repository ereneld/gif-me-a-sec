# Privacy Policy: Gif Me A Sec

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Gif Me A Sec is a Chrome extension that plays a GIF on your webcam for a few seconds during web meetings. It is built to collect nothing.

**In short: everything happens on your own computer.** The extension does not read the websites you visit, their URLs or their content, and it does not collect, store or send any of your data. It only works with your own camera video, inside your own browser.

## What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. We never see your camera, your meetings, your searches or the GIFs you pick.

## What stays in your browser

The following is saved only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and never leaves your device:

- the API keys you add for GIPHY or Pixabay
- your settings (play time, what happens afterwards, fit and so on)
- the GIF you last picked, so it is ready for your next meeting
- recent Pixabay search results, kept for up to 24 hours as Pixabay's API terms require

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## Your camera

Camera video is processed entirely inside the meeting tab on your device. When a GIF plays, its frames replace your camera frames for a few seconds. Video is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere by the extension; it goes only to the meeting site you are already using, exactly as it would without the extension.

## Third-party services

When you search, your search words and your own API key for that service are sent directly from your browser to it: [GIPHY](https://support.giphy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032872931-GIPHY-Privacy-Policy) or [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/). Thumbnails and GIFs are downloaded from their servers. Those services handle that data under their own privacy policies. If you paste a link, the GIF is downloaded from that site.

## Permissions

- **Site access, asked for after install**: installing requests no site access. You choose where it works (all sites by default, a list of meeting sites, or your own) and Chrome asks then; you can change it in Settings → Sites. It is needed only to filter the camera inside those pages. It does not read page content, URLs or browsing history, and nothing from those pages is collected or sent anywhere.
- **Storage / unlimited storage**: to keep your API keys, settings and the picked GIF (or converted video clip) on your device.
- **GIPHY and Pixabay**: asked for when you save a key for them, to search and download GIFs.
